01 / Research Object

Anonymous communication under dynamic conditions.

03 / Intended Destination

The destination is verifiability, not a slogan.

The intended state of Shye is a “verifiable research protocol”.

To become a verifiable research protocol, supported by a rigorous paper, a reference prototype and reproducible experiments that others can question, test and extend.

Theory, engineering and experiment must correspond to one another. If one part is incomplete, the other two cannot stand in as evidence for it.

04 / Research Scope

Claims and assumptions, made public together.

The paper focuses on the protocol core and states both the conditions behind its properties and the capabilities they do not establish.

  • It is not a production protocol or a deployable service.
  • The manuscript states eligibility soundness, transcript-relative honest selection, rooted authorization continuity and bounded honest propagation, with a limited scope.
  • These properties do not establish end-to-end anonymity or guaranteed delivery. Deployment-specific admission, randomness, privacy and performance require separate mechanisms and evaluation.
  • The transparent state proof still grows linearly with transitions; a bounded receipt window does not imply a constant-size proof.

This scope is summarized from the public record. Read the manuscript for complete definitions, assumptions and arguments.
