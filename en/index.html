01 / Identity

Research comes before answers.

Miroon Research is the frontier-computing research division of Miroon Studio. We work on questions that have no standard answer yet but deserve precise description.

Research frames questions, builds evidence and states the limits of its claims. When an outcome becomes mature enough, Studio can support its engineering and productisation. The two share values while carrying responsibility for different stages.

03 / Current Research

Current research and open writing.

Research projects retain strict disclosure boundaries. Open technical writing records how we understand problems, build concepts and revisit practice.

Shye

Shye is an ongoing study of anonymous communication protocols. It considers networks whose relationships do not remain fixed and whose conditions keep changing, asking how a strictly centreless communication architecture can be defined, evaluated and implemented.

04 / Method

From a question to reviewable evidence.

The aim is not an attractive conclusion. The standard is whether every step can be explained, inspected and repeated.

  1. 01Question

    Define the research question that actually needs answering.

  2. 02Assumptions

    Expose the conditions so no claim outruns its premises.

  3. 03Model

    Translate the object, environment and adversary into an analysable description.

  4. 04Experiment

    Use prototypes and experiments to test what the model can explain.

  5. 05Review

    Look deliberately for counterexamples, limitations and uncovered cases.

  6. 06Release

    Publish a version others can verify only when the materials are ready.

05 / Principles

Freedom without abandoning responsibility.
